Introduction

Welcome to the Zodiac Token Whitepaper. Zodiac Token (CZ) is a revolutionary blockchain project built on the Shibarium network, aiming to bring the ancient wisdom of the Chinese Zodiac into the decentralized world. Our mission is to create a unique ecosystem where users can interact with zodiac-themed tokens, participate in staking, and engage in a vibrant community.

Zodiac Token combines cultural heritage with cutting-edge blockchain technology, offering a seamless and rewarding experience for users worldwide. Whether you're an investor, a collector, or a blockchain enthusiast, Zodiac Token has something special for you.

Technical Overview

Zodiac Token operates on the Shibarium network (chainId: 109), a layer-2 scaling solution for Ethereum that offers low transaction fees and high scalability. Our smart contracts are written in Solidity and have been thoroughly audited to ensure security and reliability.

  • Core Contract: The Zodiac Token contract (CZ) allows users to mint CZ tokens by depositing sets of 12 zodiac-themed tokens (Rat, Ox, Tiger, etc.).
  • Token Standard: All tokens follow the ERC-20 standard, ensuring compatibility with major wallets and exchanges.
  • Staking Mechanism: Users can stake CZ tokens to earn rewards and participate in governance.
  • Interoperability: Built on Shibarium, Zodiac Token is fully interoperable with other Ethereum-based applications.
