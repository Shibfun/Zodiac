CZ Whitepaper

1. Introduction

CZ (Chinese Zodiac Token) is a unique token built on the Shibarium network, inspired by the ancient Chinese Zodiac system. The Chinese Zodiac, consisting of 12 animal signs—Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig—has been a cultural cornerstone for centuries. CZ brings this rich heritage into the blockchain space, creating a seamless mechanism for users to mint and burn tokens by interacting with zodiac-themed assets.

This whitepaper outlines the vision, mechanics, technical implementation, and potential use cases of CZ, providing a comprehensive guide for users, collectors, and stakeholders in the Shiba Inu ecosystem.

2. Token Overview

CZ is an ERC20-compatible token that represents a fixed combination of 12 zodiac-themed tokens: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig. Users can mint CZ by depositing a predefined set of these tokens and burn CZ to redeem the underlying assets, ensuring a transparent and trustless process.

3. Token Mechanics

CZ operates on a straightforward mint-and-burn mechanism, allowing users to:

  • Mint CZ: Deposit 1 set of zodiac tokens (1 Rat, 1 Ox, 1 Tiger, 1 Rabbit, 1 Dragon, 1 Snake, 1 Horse, 1 Goat, 1 Monkey, 1 Rooster, 1 Dog, 1 Pig) to receive 1 CZ.
  • Burn CZ: Redeem 1 CZ to retrieve 1 set of zodiac tokens (1 Rat, 1 Ox, 1 Tiger, 1 Rabbit, 1 Dragon, 1 Snake, 1 Horse, 1 Goat, 1 Monkey, 1 Rooster, 1 Dog, 1 Pig).

The exchange ratio is fixed at 1:1 (1 set = 1 CZ), ensuring predictability and fairness. All 12 zodiac tokens are ERC20 tokens, and the minting/burning process is executed on the Shibarium network.

4. Technical Implementation

The CZ smart contract is deployed on Shibarium, leveraging Solidity for robust and secure functionality. Key features include:

  • ERC20 Compliance: Supports standard functions (`transfer`, `approve`, `transferFrom`) for seamless integration with wallets and dApps.
  • Deposit Function: Accepts 12 zodiac tokens, mints CZ, and enforces balance and allowance checks.
  • Withdraw Function: Burns CZ and returns the 12 zodiac tokens, with strict balance validation.

5. Tokenomics

CZ's tokenomics are designed to align with the Shiba Inu ecosystem and celebrate the Chinese Zodiac culture:

  • Total Supply: Capped at 1,000,000,000,000,000 CZ, minted only through deposits.
  • Mint/Burn Ratio: Fixed at 1 set (1 of each zodiac token) = 1 CZ.
  • No Fees: The contract does not impose additional fees beyond Shibarium gas costs (paid in BONE).
  • Transparency: All minting and burning activities are recorded on-chain, verifiable via Shibarium Scan.

The fixed ratio ensures that CZ maintains a stable relationship with its underlying zodiac tokens, fostering trust and cultural engagement.
